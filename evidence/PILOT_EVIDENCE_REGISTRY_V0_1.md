## Editorial safeguards and known gaps
- SRC-0001 mixes Trần Thọ's main text with angle-bracketed later notes. Extract **only** the base-text span for EVD-0003.
- Quoted speech in historical works is **reported speech**, not a verified verbatim recording; never present as stenographic fact.
- Historical sources use polemical labels such as 賊; editorial narration should avoid reproducing these as neutral fact.
- Exact day/month for several events, dates for the 168–183 opening episode, cross-check with 本紀, and alternate accounts remain pending.
- Evidence for the nine proposed wiki biographies is **not complete**; no wiki page is approved.
- Confirm rights and attribution requirements before storing large source passages.
