### Distinguish text and annotations
- SRC-0001 includes **Pei Songzhi commentary** in angle brackets; e.g. the purported Cao Cao remark about He Jin summoning outside generals is introduced as 《魏書》曰 in commentary, NOT Chen Shou's base narration. Do not attribute the quote to Chen Shou or present it as a verified recording.
- SRC-0004 includes commentary citing 《典略》 and 《山陽公載記》. These are additional textual attestations, not Fan Ye's base narrative.
- SRC-0001 Pei commentary also preserves **multiple conflicting accounts** of Cao Cao and Lü Boshe's household, including versions attributed to 《魏書》, 《世語》, and 孫盛雜記. The celebrated phrase 「寧我負人，毋人負我」 belongs to the 孫盛雜記 version quoted in Pei's notes, **not the unannotated Chen Shou text**. This is a high-value inline comparison candidate for the next arc; do not claim all accounts agree.
