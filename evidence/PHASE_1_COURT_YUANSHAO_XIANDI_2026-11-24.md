## Source criticism
- 《三國志》卷6 袁紹 biography contains **Pei Songzhi comments** comparing conflicting family-lineage stories and questioning a theatrical Dong Zhuo–Yuan Shao dialogue attributed to 《獻帝春秋》. **Do not present that detailed literary-sounding exchange as established verbatim fact.** The basic disagreement over deposition is in Chen Shou's base text.
- 《後漢書》卷67 authorial evaluations (e.g. labels for party figures) are ancient historiography, not neutral contemporary polling.
- 《後漢書》卷9 has bracketed Tang-era annotations. Preserve base/annotation boundary.
- Lunar date 九月甲戌 is source text; **do not convert to Gregorian day without verified calendrical conversion**.
- Independent historical corroboration, editions and rights remain outstanding.
