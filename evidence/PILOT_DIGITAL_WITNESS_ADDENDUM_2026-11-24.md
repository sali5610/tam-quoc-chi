## Findings and editorial corrections
1. **Liu Bei belongs in the research scope for EP-0002**: direct source evidence exists, but this does not justify a fictional Peach Garden oath. The original nine-character wiki pilot remains unchanged unless PO approves expansion.
2. **Sun Jian chronology caution**: the first dated rebellion in the Wu 1 biography is **172**, not the Yellow Turban uprising of 184. Research his later campaigns separately.
3. The Lingdi annal's Yellow Turban number 「三十六萬」 is a source-reported figure, not independently measured demographic data.
4. *Hou Hanshu* and *Zizhi Tongjian* are different works, but the later chronicle may rely on earlier histories; do not call that independent corroboration without checking source dependence.
5. All quotation excerpts here are short and for research indexing; confirm license/edition before reproducing substantial Chinese passages in app.
