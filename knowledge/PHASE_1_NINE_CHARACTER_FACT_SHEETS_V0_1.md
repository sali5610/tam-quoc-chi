# Phase 1 — Nine character fact sheets (research-only)
Date: 2026-11-24. This is a **structured extraction of existing research**, not nine completed historical biographies. A link to a biography is not proof of every biographical detail.

## CHR-0001 Hán Linh Đế (漢靈帝)
- Identity: Eastern Han emperor; accession recorded in 168.
- Attested: accession (EVD-0015/17), 168 court crisis (EVD-0016/18).
- Direct witness: 《後漢書》卷8 靈帝紀 https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/後漢書/卷8
- Research gap: policies and changes during 169–183, independent context and precise dates.

## CHR-0002 Trương Giác (張角)
- Identity: leader associated with Yellow Turban uprising in 184.
- Attested: uprising EVD-0001/9, organizational account EVD-0032.
- Direct witness: 《後漢書》卷71 皇甫嵩朱儁列傳 https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/後漢書/卷71
- Gap: claims about magic, religious organization and troop counts need source attribution, not narrator endorsement.

## CHR-0003 Hoàng Phủ Tung (皇甫嵩)
- Identity: Han commander fighting Yellow Turbans.
- Attested: appointment EVD-0002/33, Changshe campaign EVD-0035.
- Direct witness: 《後漢書》卷71 https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/後漢書/卷71
- Gap: later career and exact campaign ordering.

## CHR-0004 Chu Tuấn (朱儁)
- Identity: Han commander in Yellow Turban campaigns.
- Attested: military appointment EVD-0033, setback against Bo Cai EVD-0034, Sun Jian's appointment EVD-0021.
- Direct witness: 《後漢書》卷71 https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/後漢書/卷71 ; 《三國志》卷46 https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/三國志/卷46
- Gap: standalone biography section, detailed campaign and geographic collation.

## CHR-0005 Tào Tháo (曹操)
- Identity: early career official and military commander.
- Attested: appointment to fight rebels EVD-0003, role in Changshe EVD-0035, raising forces in 189 per Wei 1.
- Direct witness: 《三國志》卷1 武帝紀 https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/三國志/卷01
- Variant caution: Lü Boshe household accounts and famous maxim in Pei-cited works, not Chen Shou's unannotated base text.
- Gap: precise career transition and episode placement.

## CHR-0006 Hà Tiến (何進)
- Identity: Eastern Han general, central to 184 and 189.
- Attested: appointment EVD-0019, summons Dong Zhuo EVD-0004, assassination EVD-0005.
- Direct witness: 《後漢書》卷69 何進 https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/後漢書/卷69
- Gap: conflicting palace-crisis details and source dependencies.

## CHR-0007 Viên Thiệu (袁紹)
- Identity: court figure in He Jin's plan and Dong Zhuo succession conflict.
- Attested: EVD-0027/28/29.
- Direct witness: 《三國志》卷6 袁紹 https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/三國志/卷06
- Variant caution: Pei comments on theatrical conversation attributed to 《獻帝春秋》; do not quote as certain historical dialogue.
- Gap: family lineage variant collation and subsequent chronology.

## CHR-0008 Đổng Trác (董卓)
- Identity: military leader; role in failed Yellow Turban campaign and 189 succession.
- Attested: failed campaign EVD-0036, emperor replacement EVD-0006.
- Direct witness: 《三國志》卷6 董卓 https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/三國志/卷06 ; 《後漢書》卷72 https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/後漢書/卷72
- Gap: 189 troop movement, timing and contradictory reports.

## CHR-0009 Hán Hiến Đế (漢獻帝)

- Direct witness: 《後漢書》卷9 孝獻帝紀 https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/後漢書/卷9
