1. **EP-0001 — Triều đình trước cơn bão (168–183)**: court and political structures; avoid simplistic 'eunuchs alone caused collapse'. Needs direct Hou Hanshu passage verification and other context.
2. **EP-0002 — Khăn Vàng: cuộc nổi dậy năm 184**: Zhang Jiao, scale of mobilization, imperial response, roles of Huangfu Song, Zhu Jun and Cao Cao; do not assert a fictional oath of brotherhood. Source anchor Hou Hanshu Huangfu Song/Zhu Jun biography, cross-reference Sanguozhi Wei 1.
3. **EP-0003 — Năm 189: kinh thành đổi chủ**: Lingdi's death, He Jin's plan, eunuch crisis, Dong Zhuo's arrival and succession intervention; coalition of 190 only as transition to next arc. Source anchor Sanguozhi Wei 1; further compare Hou Hanshu He Jin and Dong Zhuo biographies.
