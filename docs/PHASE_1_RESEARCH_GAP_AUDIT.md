## Verification protocol
1. Open digital witness and record its **exact URL and edition statement**.
2. Transcribe a short exact excerpt with paragraph/juan locator and identify whether base text, commentary, or later editor.
3. Independently compare with another witness of the same passage; same underlying work is not independent historical corroboration.
4. Check date conversion, actors and contradictions; assign claim confidence separately from source category.
5. Record reproduction permissions before storing substantial text.
6. Reviewer approves EVD record before use in public-facing prose.
