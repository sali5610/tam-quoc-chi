# Story illustration direction — PO approved 2026-11-24

Mandatory for every current and future episode: **Tam Quốc Chí Đồ Tượng (三國志圖像), 1644–1735 edition, is the first-choice event illustration source.** Use its authentic woodblock art as the visual identity. Search the complete edition before choosing supplemental art. Prefer its illustrations as hero and primary scene art; other editions, museum objects, maps and sequential illustrations fill missing beats or add useful detail. Do not replace a matching primary illustration with supplemental art merely because it is easier to fetch. Preserve original ink/paper, crop scan margins without altering artwork, caption in Vietnamese and retain exact page provenance. Mark literary depictions as such; they are not evidence of historical events. Place images after the relevant beat, avoid spoilers, keep assets bundled locally. No repeated network downloads on build. Source Library mirror: https://sourcelibrary.org/zh/book/sanguo-zhi-tuxiang-illustrated-romance-of-the-three-kingdoms . Existing four recovered WebPs remain supplementary. The review branch has eight local files, including four primary-edition files representing five distinct scenes. Build and browser display must be separately verified.
